# Design experiments (generated audio only)

Measurements behind specific config choices. All on audio generated by
`evals/synth.py` (the test pack was not available). Scripts were throwaway
(`.cache/scratch/`); the numbers are copied here so the reasoning is auditable.

## E1. Downbeat cue: network only vs network + harmonic change (2026-10-12)
31 generated cases from the robustness suite, downbeat F (+-70 ms) of the decoded bars.

| subset | network only | network x chroma-change (geometric, w=0.5) |
|---|---|---|
| 3/4 cases (7) | 3 correct meter; 4 read as 4/4 (downbeat F 0.29-0.33) | 5 correct; 1 still 4/4; 1 read as 6/8 (F 0.67) |
| 4/4 cases (19) | all 4/4 | all 4/4, no downbeat F changed |
| 6/8 cases (5) | hypermeter: network marks every 2nd bar (F 0.67) | unchanged |

Adopted `DOWNBEAT_NETWORK_WEIGHT = 0.5` because it fixed 2 of 4 wrong 3/4 cases and changed no other
case. Caveat: generated chords change exactly on bar lines, which flatters the chroma cue.

## E2. Subdivision (6/8 vs 4/4) feature
Ratio of onset energy at beat-thirds vs beat-half, same 31 cases:
- full-band flux, 1024-pt frames: 6/8 0.98-1.93 vs swung 4/4 1.23 -> overlapping, unusable.
- full-band flux, 256-pt frames: everything 0.9-1.1 -> unusable.
- **flux above 4 kHz, 512-pt frames**: binary meters 0.02-0.24; compound at dotted-quarter level 4.4-11.3;
  outliers were swung 4/4 (4.0) and an octave-doubled 60 BPM track (1.9), both caused by dividing by an
  empty half-beat. Fixed by normalising both terms by on-beat energy and requiring triplet energy at both
  thirds (see `meter.subdivision_evidence`).
